More Than a Ride. It's the Journey.

Mtemi Swift Rides is a Nakuru-based transport brand built around comfortable travel, reliable service and unforgettable journeys.

Our story

Built around the journey, not just the destination

Mtemi Swift Rides provides passenger transportation and van hire for individuals, families, companies, churches, wedding parties, running clubs, hiking groups, tourists and organized groups moving across Kenya and beyond.

The brand is driven by comfort, reliability, customer satisfaction, adventure, professional service, group travel and Kenya's own road-trip culture — the belief that a good journey is worth as much as a good destination.
